Spiritual Illusions

The Moving Wall

Perceiving the Imperceptible

This illusion presents a static image that appears to flow and shift before your eyes, much like how 1 Kings 19:11-12 teaches us about perceiving the Spirit through stillness rather than dramatic displays. Vertical bands of subtle color gradients create a false sense of motion, reminding us of Helaman 5:30 and how the voice of the Lord comes as a whisper that pierces the soul. As you gaze at the wall, you may notice different sections seeming to drift in opposing directions, revealing the constructed nature of perception itself. The Lord speaks to us not through spectacular signs but through D&C 85:6 —that still small voice which whispers through and pierceth all things. We must train our spiritual eyes to perceive this subtle guidance, as Alma 5:46 teaches that we must seek through fasting and prayer to know spiritual truths. The Spirit confirms truth to our minds and hearts as promised in D&C 8:2-3 , speaking to both mind and heart. Like Moroni teaches in Moroni 10:4-5 , we can know the truth of all things by the power of the Holy Ghost when we ask with sincere heart and real intent. The mysteries of God are revealed through spiritual eyes opened by the power of the Spirit, as Moses 1:11-12 testifies—Moses beheld God not with natural eyes but with spiritual eyes. Even when truths seem sealed to the learned, as described in 2 Nephi 27:5 , they are made plain to those who seek with humility and faith.

The Living Light

The Pulse of Divinity

A single circle pulses with rhythmic expansion and contraction, mimicking the breath of a living being—much like D&C 88:6-13 teaches that the Light of Christ is in all things and gives life to all things. Through carefully timed scaling and brightness changes, this static shape evokes the essence of life and consciousness. The concentric rings that emanate outward suggest energy radiating from a central source, like John 1:9 D&C 50:24-25 promises that light grows brighter and brighter until the perfect day. We are commanded to let our light so shine before men, as Matthew 5:14-16 instructs, that others may see our good works and glorify our Father in heaven. The light we share helps others find their way, as taught in 3 Nephi 18:24 Moroni 7:16 teaches, inviting all to do good and believe in Christ. This light never completely disappears—it only changes intensity based on our receptivity, much like the breath of life God breathed into Adam as recorded in Moses 3:7 . We have time to prepare to meet God, as Alma 34:32-34 urgently reminds us, for after this life comes the night of darkness where no labor can be performed. The spirits of the righteous are received into paradise, as Alma 40:11 teaches, taken home to that God who gave them life.

The Divine Mystery

Beyond Human Understanding

This impossible object defies the laws of three-dimensional space, presenting a square that cannot exist in physical reality. Through clever use of perspective and shading, the shape appears to connect in ways that contradict itself when examined closely. Such mysteries remind us that Isaiah 55:8-9 —God's thoughts are higher than our thoughts and His ways higher than our ways. The illusion represents those truths that transcend human logic, much like Nephi's declaration in 1 Nephi 1:1 that he had great knowledge of the mysteries of God. Jacob teaches that Jacob 4:8 —it is impossible for man to find out all God's ways unless revealed. Some truths remain mysterious to men but known to God. Even when we cannot understand, we can trust in God's perfect knowledge, as Ammon testified in Alma 26:21-22 —the penitent in heart are given to know the mysteries of God. The mysteries of godliness are understood only by power, as D&C 76:114-118 teaches—only to be seen and understood by the power of the Holy Spirit. Moses beheld worlds without number created by the Son, as recorded in Moses 1:30-31 1 Corinthians 2:9-11 promises—yet God reveals them by His Spirit. If we ask, we shall receive revelation, as promised in D&C 42:61 2 Nephi 32:7 teaches—great knowledge given in plainness to those who search.

Layers of Truth

Revelation Line Upon Line
REVELATION REVELATION REVELATION

Three identical words stacked upon each other create the illusion of depth and dimension through shadow and offset positioning. Each layer recedes into virtual space, suggesting that truth comes gradually, built upon previous understanding—exactly as 2 Nephi 28:30 teaches—line upon line, precept upon precept, here a little and there a little. The effect mirrors how revelation arrives, just as Isaiah 28:10 declares. We are proven in all things and must treasure up wisdom, as commanded in D&C 98:12 D&C 50:40 . Learning is good if we hearken unto the counsels of God, as 2 Nephi 9:28-29 teaches—to be learned is good if we hearken unto God's counsels. The mysteries of God are given according to our heed and diligence, as Alma 12:9-10 explains—he who hardens his heart receives the lesser portion, while he who does not harden his heart receives the greater portion until he knows the mysteries in full. Truth is knowledge of things as they really are, as D&C 93:28 teaches, and those who keep commandments receive truth and light until glorified. The Spirit will show us all things we should do, as promised in 2 Nephi 32:6 . God gives knowledge by His Holy Spirit, as D&C 121:26-28 testifies—knowledge by the unspeakable gift of the Holy Ghost, a time when nothing shall be withheld.

The Field of Agency

Your Influence on Reality

This interactive illusion responds to your presence, warping and bending as you move across its surface. Your cursor becomes a force of influence, demonstrating how individual actions ripple through the fabric of existence. This represents the profound truth of 2 Nephi 2:27 —we are free to choose liberty and eternal life or captivity and death. Agency is so precious that Moses 4:3 Joshua 24:15 declares—as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord. We are not passive observers but active participants whose choices shape reality around us. Our words will condemn us, as Alma 12:14 warns—our words, works, and thoughts will condemn us. All things are governed by law, as D&C 132:5 teaches—we must abide the law appointed for each blessing. Whatsoever a man sows, that shall he also reap, as Galatians 6:7 testifies—God is not mocked. Blessings are predicated upon obedience to law, as D&C 130:20-21 declares. Wickedness never was happiness, as Alma 41:10 firmly states. We are redeemed from the fall and become free forever, as 2 Nephi 2:26 teaches—free to act for ourselves and not to be acted upon, knowing good from evil.

The Eternal Round

Progress Without End

Eight rings advance endlessly toward the viewer, creating the sensation of infinite forward motion through space. Each ring follows the same path yet remains eternally distant, suggesting progress without destination. This evokes God's work and glory to bring to pass Moses 1:39 —the immortality and eternal life of man. We can become gods, as promised in D&C 132:19-20 —then shall they be gods, because they have no end. He that overcometh shall inherit all things, as Revelation 21:7 promises. This life is our time to prepare to meet God, as Alma 34:32-34 urgently teaches. There are things to act and things to be acted upon, as 2 Nephi 2:15 explains. The greater governs the lesser, as Abraham 3:18-19 teaches regarding the organization of intelligences. If we keep God's commandments, we shall receive of His fulness, as D&C 93:20 promises—grace for grace. We can become partakers of the divine nature, as 2 Peter 1:4 testifies. We must come unto Christ and be perfected in Him, as Moroni 10:32-33 invites—come unto Christ, deny yourselves of all ungodliness, love God with all your might, mind and strength, and be perfected in Him.
